Saigon Purple · The Story

MEET PURPLE

Purple is the private atelier of Harry — a one-person studio in Saigon devoted to the rarest velvet Anthurium. What began with a single leaf on a windowsill is now a climate-controlled room where love and luxury grow in every vein.

01 — The beginning

One velvet leaf

It started with a single Carlablackiae leaf — deep, near-black velvet that seemed to drink the light rather than reflect it. One leaf became a shelf; a shelf became a room.

Somewhere along the way the hobby turned into an obsession, and the obsession became Purple: a place to raise aroids the slow, careful way they deserve.

02 — The approach

Veinclusive, not hype

Every specimen is chosen by hand for vein, velvet and form — never by trend. The philosophy is simple: stability over speed. We don’t force big leaves or chase fast growth.

We hold a tight, patient envelope and let each plant become the best version of itself. Fewer plants, better plants — a veinclusive purple selection.

03 — The setup

The room, like an instrument

Everything is measured, logged and kept consistent — because rare plants reward the keeper who shows up every day.
